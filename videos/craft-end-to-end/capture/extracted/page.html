Universal Institutional Failures

The blindspots CRAFT was built to expose.

Abstracted from fiduciary assessments across the globe, the same structural gaps recur regardless of geography or mandate.

The Spreadsheet Trap

Legacy accounting systems unable to generate automated donor-specific trial balances.

The Sub-National Blindspot

Centralized logistics systems with critically low facility-level reporting.

The Paper Compliance Illusion

Institutions with perfect policy manuals but zero system-enforced implementation.

The Anonymity Gap

Whistleblowing mechanisms requiring staff names, deterring fraud reporting.

A four-layer lifecycle, from intent to execution.

01

Assessment

Diagnose institutional readiness across 5 tiers and 20 domains using a 0–5 maturity scale backed by a 7-point evidence matrix.

02

Accreditation

Compute a Level A–E rating. The Paper Compliance Penalty Engine caps the level when implementation evidence is weak.

03

Strengthening

Convert every gap into a costed, owned, and time-bound action within a Capacity Improvement Plan scheduled to your own chosen timeframe.

04

Monitoring

Track validated execution over time, re-scoring as evidence matures and donor frameworks evolve.
