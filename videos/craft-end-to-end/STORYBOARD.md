## Video direction

- palette system: canvas = cream (#FFFFFF) with a quiet cream-2 tint for panels; ink (#1E293B) for body copy; pink-deep (#004F3B, CRAFT deep green) for display type and the hero figure; green / green-lite for rules, bars and positive states; one warm alarm accent ONLY for loss in frames 2-3 and the tagline word "Africa" in frame 11 — the brand gold (#FFB900) — never red. Frames 1-3 (the problem) sit on a cream-2 tint for gravity; frames 4-10 on clean cream. Frame 11 inverts: pink-deep ground, cream type.
- type: display ramp (Inter 500, tight tracking) for claims and figures, `stat-figure` for numbers, JetBrains Mono `label` / `caption-mono` uppercase for chrome, sources and tags. Every frame carries the topbar spine: mono label left ("CRAFT · ICARF v4.0"), frame counter right ("01 / 11").
- citation chrome: frames 1-3 put the source on screen as a mono `caption-mono` line with a 2px green rule above, sitting at ~78% height (above the caption band), revealed when the stat lands. It is the proof, never decoration.
- motion grammar: long-tail power3 eases, smooth over bouncy; every piece reveals on its spoken cue (word timings are given in each Scene as @seconds, frame-local); count-ups run while the number is spoken; hairline rules draw on left to right before the text they underline. The soundtrack is drum-led: land key reveals on the word, not on arbitrary beats.
- rhythm: frames 1-3 slow and grave (fewer, heavier reveals); 4 is the turn (kinetic); 5-8 brisk product beats; 9 warm and wide; 10 a calm held CTA (breather: reveals then holds still); 11 a held brand lockup.
- negative list: no "DiBadili" text anywhere (the institute logo artwork in frame 9 is the sole, approved exception); no stock icons standing in for product UI; reconstructed UI uses real CRAFT labels only; no purple/blue AI gradients, no bokeh, no drop shadows (flat paper depth); no slideshow (front-load then freeze) and no screensaver (independent floating). Nothing important in the bottom ~17% caption band.
