## Intent

Second CRAFT launch film, a completely different version from craft-end-to-end. Male, middle-aged,
experienced, authoritative, polished baritone narrator — user chose Abeo (Nigerian English), deepened.
Data and problem focused on Nigeria, noted as similar across Africa and the Global South: FDI volatility,
slow and rigorous development finance, the venture-capital gap, yet Nigeria's unicorns and strong
institutions — "imagine if trust, fiduciary accountability and transparency could be proven."
Then the step-by-step product: onboarding, entering details, the assessment workflow across the
organisation, the science and logic, reports and results, tracking progress and how you, your funders
and independent reviewers can review it. Built for institutions big and small; saves time and resources;
building local competencies for global relevance. Plan approved in chat (2026-10-11); sketches skipped.
