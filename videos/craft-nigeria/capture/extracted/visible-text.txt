[p] Universal Institutional Failures
[h2] The blindspots CRAFT was built to expose.
[p] Abstracted from fiduciary assessments across the globe, the same structural gaps recur regardless of geography or mandate.
[h3] The Spreadsheet Trap
[p] Legacy accounting systems unable to generate automated donor-specific trial balances.
[h3] The Sub-National Blindspot
[p] Centralized logistics systems with critically low facility-level reporting.
[h3] The Paper Compliance Illusion
[p] Institutions with perfect policy manuals but zero system-enforced implementation.
[h3] The Anonymity Gap
[p] Whistleblowing mechanisms requiring staff names, deterring fraud reporting.
[p] One framework, three institutional realities
[h2] Built for every institution in the trust chain.
[p] A universal fiduciary core applies to all institutions. CRAFT then adapts the questions, evidence and Data Room to your organizational archetype - so you are measured against the standard that actually governs your funding.

[h2] A four-layer lifecycle, from intent to execution.
[h3] Assessment
[p] Diagnose institutional readiness across 5 tiers and 20 domains using a 0–5 maturity scale backed by a 7-point evidence matrix.
[h3] Accreditation
[p] Compute a Level A–E rating. The Paper Compliance Penalty Engine caps the level when implementation evidence is weak.
[h3] Strengthening
[p] Convert every gap into a costed, owned, and time-bound action within a Capacity Improvement Plan scheduled to your own chosen timeframe.
[h3] Monitoring
[p] Track validated execution over time, re-scoring as evidence matures and donor frameworks evolve.
