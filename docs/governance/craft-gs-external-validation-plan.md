# External validation plan — CRAFT Governance Scorecard (CRAFT-GS 1.0)

Status: plan for approval · Prepared 15 October 2026 · Owner: DiBadili Institute methodology lead

## Purpose

CRAFT-GS 1.0 (10 pillars, 30 core and 4 innovation-support criteria, 1–5 rubric, evidence cap of 2, approved 12 October 2026) is used to describe institutions to funders. Before results are relied on outside the institution being assessed, an independent panel should test whether the methodology:

1. **measures what it claims** (content validity);
2. **gives the same result when different assessors apply it** (reliability);
3. **agrees with other evidence** about governance quality (criterion validity);
4. **is not driven by arbitrary weights** (sensitivity).

This is validation of the *methodology*. It is separate from the independent validation of an individual *assessment*, which the platform already supports.

## Scope

- Pillars, pillar weights (12/10/10/12/11/9/9/9/9/9) and rating bands.
- The 34 criteria, their questions, evidence requirements and recommended actions.
- The rubric, the evidence cap, N/A handling and weight renormalisation.
- The founder-protection overlay (ETH-4, DAT-4, ACC-4, RSK-4), to be reviewed together with legal wording (see the review pack).

Out of scope: certification, credit rating or equivalence with any national corporate-governance code.

## Panel and independence

| Role | Number | Profile |
|---|---|---|
| Chair | 1 | Senior governance or evaluation expert with no CRAFT role |
| Practitioners | 3 | Board/company secretaries or internal auditors from at least two countries (e.g. Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana) |
| Funders | 2 | DFI or donor fiduciary-risk specialists |
| Public sector | 1 | Supreme audit institution or public-financial-management specialist |
| Methods | 1 | Statistician or psychometrician |
| Observer | 1 | Regulator or professional body (non-voting) |

Each member signs a declaration of interests; anyone who built CRAFT-GS or is paid by an institution in the pilot sample cannot vote. Records of panel decisions are kept with the methodology version.

## Method

| Step | What | Test and threshold |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Content review | Each panellist rates every criterion for relevance and clarity (4-point scale) | Item content validity index ≥ 0.78; criteria below are revised or removed |
| 2. Weights (Delphi) | Two to three anonymous rounds on pillar weights, with reasons | Stop when the inter-quartile range per pillar ≤ 2 points; compare with current weights |
| 3. Pilot assessments | 12–20 institutions across at least 3 countries and 4 types (ministry, SOE, NGO, private/listed, DFI-funded programme) | Mix of sizes; consent and data agreements in place |
| 4. Reliability | Two trained assessors score each pilot institution independently, from the same evidence | Krippendorff’s alpha ≥ 0.67 overall (≥ 0.80 target); criteria below 0.6 are rewritten |
| 5. Criterion validity | Compare CRAFT-GS scores with independent signals: audit opinions and management-letter findings, PEFA/APRM results where available, funder risk ratings | Expected direction of association; documented cases where they disagree |
| 6. Sensitivity | Recompute ratings with ±20% weight changes and equal weights | Rating band changes for < 10% of institutions; otherwise revisit weights |
| 7. Usability | Time to complete, evidence burden, assessor and institution feedback | Median completion time and pain points reported |

## Outputs and decision

- A validation report with each test result, panel recommendations and dissent.
- A change log. **Any change to weights, criteria, bands or the rubric creates CRAFT-GS 1.1**; past assessments keep their version and are not rescored silently.
- A public methodology note stating what was validated, with whom, and the limits.

## Resources

Panel honoraria, assessor time for double scoring, translation (French and Portuguese pilots), and analysis time. Costs are to be estimated by the programme team; none are assumed here.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Too few pilot institutions | Partner with professional bodies and DFIs early; accept 12 as the minimum |
| Evidence not shareable with assessors | Use the platform’s access controls and data agreements; anonymise results |
| Panel dominated by one country or sector | Balance by design; record dissent |
| Results contradict current weights | Treat as a finding; change only through a new version |
