## 2. Is there a pan-African equivalent of the GDPR?

**No single registration or regulator covers Africa.**

- **AU Malabo Convention** (Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection) entered into force on **8 June 2023** after its 15th ratification. It sets minimum principles that ratifying states must put into national law. It has no continental register, regulator or enforcement, and it does not bind organisations directly.
- **Regional instruments** (ECOWAS Supplementary Act on personal data protection, 2010; SADC Model Law on data protection, 2013) align principles within regions, but registration stays national.
- **Practical approach:** run **one privacy programme to the highest common standard** (NDPA plus GDPR-style principles: lawful basis, minimisation, rights, security, transfer safeguards, breach notification, DPO). Our privacy policies, data processing terms and sub-processor list now follow this. Then **register country by country** where we target users or set up operations.
