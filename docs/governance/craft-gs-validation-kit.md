## 3. Content-validity rating sheet (step 1)

Export the criteria from the Methodology page. Each panellist rates every criterion independently.

|---|---|---|---|

**Scoring:** item content validity index (I-CVI) = share of panellists rating relevance 3 or 4. Keep at ≥ 0.78; revise or remove below. Record the clarity comments with each revision.

>
