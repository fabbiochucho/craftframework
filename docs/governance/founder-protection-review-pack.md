# Founder-protection overlay — wording review pack

For: legal counsel and programme partners · Prepared 15 October 2026 · Methodology: CRAFT-GS 1.0

## Why this review

The CRAFT Governance Scorecard adds four criteria when the assessed institution funds or supports innovators (incubators, accelerators, competitions, innovation funders). They aim to show that founders are contracted fairly, their ideas and IP are safeguarded, they can raise grievances, and someone is accountable. Because the wording may be read as describing legal duties, it must be confirmed by legal counsel and by programme partners **before results are shared outside the assessed institution**.

The criteria are a governance-maturity assessment. They are not legal advice, do not create legal obligations and do not replace a contract review.

## The four criteria (current wording)

### ETH-4 Fair and transparent founder contracting
*Equity, IP-assignment and other obligations asked of founders are documented, proportionate and agreed with informed consent.*
- Are equity terms, founder obligations and any IP assignment disclosed in writing before founders apply or commit?
- Is informed consent recorded, with time and access to independent advice before signing?
- Are terms reviewed for proportionality against the value the programme provides?
- Evidence: published terms predating applications; consent records for a sample of founders.
- Recommended action: publish standard terms before applications open, record informed consent and give founders time and access to independent advice before they sign.

### DAT-4 Intellectual property and confidential information safeguards
*Ideas, IP and confidential information submitted by every participant — not only the winners — are protected.*
- Are IP ownership and licensing terms clear for every stage of the programme?
- Are there written rules for handling submitted ideas, including for unsuccessful applicants?
- Is access to proprietary submissions restricted, logged and time-limited, with deletion or return on exit?
- Evidence: access logs for submissions; deletion or return records for unsuccessful applicants.
- Recommended action: adopt an IP and submission-handling policy covering all participants, bind judges and mentors to confidentiality, and restrict, log and expire access to submissions.

### ACC-4 Accessible grievance and dispute resolution for founders
*Founders and participants can raise complaints and have them resolved or referred within defined timelines.*
- Is there an accessible complaint channel for founders and participants, including after the programme?
- Are escalation steps and response timelines defined and met?
- Are cases documented through resolution or referral (for example to mediation or arbitration), without retaliation?
- Evidence: response and resolution times against targets; referral records.
- Recommended action: publish a grievance procedure with escalation steps and response timelines, log every case to resolution or referral, and report timeliness to the board.

### RSK-4 Demonstrable founder-safeguard accountability
*Someone owns founder safeguards; breaches are tracked as corrective actions and followed up.*
- Is an accountable owner assigned for founder safeguards?
- Are breaches of commitments to founders recorded and tracked to verified closure?
- Does the board or funder receive periodic reporting on safeguards and breaches?
- Evidence: corrective actions linked to breaches; board or funder reports.
- Recommended action: assign an owner for founder safeguards, log breaches as corrective actions with evidence-based closure, and report them to the board or funder.

## Questions for legal counsel

1. Does any wording imply a legal standard (e.g. "proportionate", "informed consent", "without retaliation") that could be read as a finding of legal non-compliance? Suggest neutral alternatives where needed.
2. Is "IP assignment" and "licensing" terminology correct across common-law (Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa) and OHADA/civil-law (Francophone) jurisdictions?
3. Are the data-handling expectations for submissions (logging, time-limited access, deletion or return) consistent with data-protection laws (e.g. NDPA 2023, Kenya DPA 2019, POPIA)?
4. Is referring to mediation or arbitration appropriate, or should the wording stay neutral ("external resolution")?
5. Should the criteria carry a standard disclaimer on reports that include them?

## Questions for programme partners

1. Are the evidence items realistic for incubators and competitions of different sizes?
2. Would the recommended actions conflict with existing funder agreements or investor terms?
3. Is one owner for safeguards realistic in small programmes, or should a role be named instead?
4. Are there founder-protection practices we have missed (for example, equity-free programmes, revenue-share terms)?

## Sign-off
