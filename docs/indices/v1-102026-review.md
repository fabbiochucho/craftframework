Scoring convention: 3 = documented and approved · 4 = operating, with records to show it · 5 = reviewed and improving.

**4 indices, 94 indicators.**

## Organisational Effectiveness Index

*Is the institution set up to deliver its strategy, and do its people make that happen?*

Twenty-four indicators across direction, leadership, design, accountability, culture, engagement, capabilities and outside focus. Each asks what the institution can show, not how people feel, so the result stands up to a funder or a validator.

Bands: Fragile from 0 · Developing from 1.75 · Functional from 2.75 · Strong from 3.5 · Exemplary from 4.4. A dimension below 1.75 caps the band at Developing.

Lenses: Strategy to work (OHE-01, OHE-02, OHE-07, OHE-10) · People to results (OHE-12, OHE-16, OHE-19, OHE-20) · Outside in (OHE-22, OHE-23, OHE-24).

## Change Readiness & Adoption Index

*Can the institution deliver a change, including its own improvement plan, so that it sticks?*

Eighteen indicators on how the institution carries out change: the case for it, who leads it, whether people adopt it, how they are supported, early results and making it last. Answer each question for the most recent major change, or for delivering the Capacity Improvement Plan if there has been none. Gaps here become plan actions about how that plan is delivered.

Bands: Fragile from 0 · Developing from 1.75 · Functional from 2.75 · Strong from 3.5 · Exemplary from 4.4. A dimension below 1.75 caps the band at Developing.

Lenses: Starting well (CHG-01, CHG-04, CHG-07, CHG-10) · Landing it (CHG-08, CHG-09, CHG-12, CHG-13) · Making it stick (CHG-16, CHG-17, CHG-18).

## How the safeguards screening works

Organisations answer seven yes/no questions before any safeguards question is shown:

1. We build, renovate or operate physical sites
2. We employ or contract more than 50 people
3. We work in a sensitive location (conflict, flood-prone areas, informal settlements)
4. We acquire land or restrict people's access to it
5. We work in or near protected or high-biodiversity areas
6. We work near cultural heritage sites
7. We lend to or invest in other organisations

- **High risk:** physical sites (1) and either a sensitive location (3) or land acquisition (4).
- **Moderate risk:** physical sites (1) or more than 50 people (2).
- **Low risk:** everyone else.
- Questions 4 to 7 also switch on the land, biodiversity, heritage and lending areas, whatever the category.
- Every organisation is asked the core system, labour, SEAH and stakeholder questions.
