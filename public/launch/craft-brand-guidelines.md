# CRAFT brand guidelines

## Name
- Write **CRAFT** in capitals. On first mention: *CRAFT: Capacity Readiness & Fiduciary Assurance Toolkit*.
- Framework version: **ICARF v4.0**. Publisher: **DiBadili Institute** ("Become Change").
- Don't use the legacy name G2G-ICGMT in launch materials.

## Logo
- **Prism shield mark**: emerald shield containing a gold-to-green delta. Use alone for avatars, favicons and small sizes.
- **Lockup**: mark + CRAFT wordmark (Playfair Display ExtraBold) + "ICARF v4.0 · DiBadili Institute".
  Use `craft-lockup-light-bg` on white/light surfaces and `craft-lockup-dark-bg` on Deep Emerald.
- **Mono mark** (white) for single-colour placements, embossing, or photos with strong contrast.
- Clear space: at least the height of the delta on every side. Minimum size: 24 px (mark), 160 px wide (lockup).
- Don't recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add drop shadows to the mark.

## Colour
| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Emerald | `#022C22` | Primary backgrounds |
| Emerald 900 | `#064E3B` | Headlines on light |
| Emerald 500 | `#10B981` | Primary actions |
| Mint | `#34D399` | Links & accents on dark |
| Delta Gold | `#FBBF24` | Highlights, dates, CTAs (accents only, never long text) |
| Slate 600 | `#475569` | Body text on light |

## Typography
- **Playfair Display** (700–800): headlines, wordmark.
- **Inter** (400–700): body copy and UI.
- **JetBrains Mono** (500–700): URLs, codes, data.

## Voice
Plain, confident, evidence-first. Talk about what institutions *do*, not what their manuals say. Avoid hype;
back claims with the framework's numbers (5 tiers, 20 domains, 0–5 maturity, 24-month plans).
Always include the URL **craftframework.becomechange.institute**.
