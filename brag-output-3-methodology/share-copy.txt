The CRAFT Framework, explained: built on ICARF v4.0, CRAFT scores institutions across 5 tiers and 20 domains, backs every answer with a 7-point evidence ladder, and caps accreditation when policy outruns practice — the Paper Compliance Penalty Engine.
Public Sector, Civil Society, and Private Sector institutions are each measured differently, with results that reflect what their actual funders care about.
